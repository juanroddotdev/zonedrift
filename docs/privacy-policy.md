# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 29, 2026

ZoneDrift ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that helps you view US time zones and plan meetings. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

## Summary

ZoneDrift stores a small amount of information **only on your device**. The extension does **not** send your data to our servers. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Data stored locally

The extension uses Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API to save:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Pinned locations | Your saved watchlist of US time zones |
| City labels | Display names you chose when pinning |
| Last search query | Restores your search when you reopen the popup |
| Time format preference | 12-hour or 24-hour clock, if enabled |
| First-run flag | Whether default pins were already applied |

This data stays in your browser profile on your device.

## Data we do not collect

ZoneDrift does **not**:

- Collect personal information on external servers
- Use cookies or third-party trackers
- Make network requests to our services
- Read browsing history, page content, or passwords
- Access your location

## Permissions

**Storage** — Required to save your pins and preferences locally. No data leaves your device because of this permission.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete all extension data at any time:

1. Open `chrome://extensions`
2. Find **ZoneDrift**
3. Click **Details**
4. Click **Clear storage** (or remove the extension)

## Children

ZoneDrift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy if the extension changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any revision.
